## Direction

Turn the frame and Creative Chaos center into one connected system rather than two nested graphics.

- The fine angular C enclosure and exact brand palette remain unchanged.
- A narrow tapered crimson conduit connects the left C's upper inner terminal to the orbit's upper-right quadrant.
- A matching narrow navy conduit connects the right C's lower inner terminal to the orbit's lower-left quadrant.
- Both bridges narrow from roughly 20 units at the frame to 10 units at the orbit so they remain connective rather than visually dominant.
- Orbit radius grows from 170 to 185.
- The orbit uses 18-unit main strokes and narrows to 10-unit approach arcs at each connection, clarifying how the bridges enter the circle.
- The spark grows from a 200-unit to a 250-unit footprint while retaining the approved four-point curvature.
- The three planets remain directly seated on the larger orbit.
