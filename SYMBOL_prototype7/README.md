## Direction

Make the central orbit the sole structural relationship between the two Cs.

- The left crimson C and reversed right navy C are now independent bodies.
- A clear 40-unit gap separates their upper terminals and another separates their lower terminals.
- The left C connects only from its upper terminal to the orbit's upper-right approach.
- Its lower terminal remains open and disconnected.
- The right C connects only from its lower terminal to the orbit's lower-left approach.
- Its upper terminal remains open and disconnected.
- Both C bodies therefore relate to each other only through the orbit.
- Orbit, planets, dominant spark, proportions and palette remain unchanged from SYMBOL prototype 6.
