## Direction

Keep the approved central spark while simplifying and opening the surrounding system.

- Both connected C bands are thinner again, with approximately one-third more internal breathing room.
- The red/navy interlocking seam and closed navy right boundary are preserved.
- The central four-point spark is unchanged from SYMBOL prototype 2.
- The orbit is now one geometrically circular, continuous crimson line.
- Three subtly different planet sizes are evenly distributed at 120-degree intervals.
- Every planet center sits directly on the orbit and the line continues beneath it, eliminating detached gaps.
- The circle, planets and spark share the exact same center.
