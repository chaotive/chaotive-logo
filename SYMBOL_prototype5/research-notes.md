# Balance research — SYMBOL prototype 5

The references were used to study hierarchy and spacing only. No external logo geometry was copied.

## Official references

- [Ubisoft: Say Hello to the New Ubisoft Swirl](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/3aNoNe5cyUPXOhSFcQJmxN/null) describes a minimalist, monochrome symbol conceived as a window into its worlds. The useful principle is that an enclosure can establish identity without becoming a heavy border.
- [Rockstar Games official avatar downloads](https://www.rockstargames.com/es/downloads?collection=6&series=80) show a compact mark in which the central R/star idea visually dominates its surrounding container. The useful principle is a clear primary motif supported by a lighter boundary.
- [Epic Games design guidelines](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/epic-online-services/accounts-and-social/eos-epic-account-services/epic-account-services-design-guidelines) emphasize preserving aspect ratio and proportional clear space around the official mark. The useful principle is to treat internal spacing as a repeatable system rather than incidental padding.
- [IO Interactive press room](https://ioi.dk/fr/press) provides official logo media and reinforces the value of a compact, consistently reproduced studio identifier.

## Chaotive conclusions

- The Creative Chaos orbit and spark should be read first.
- The opposing Cs remain important as the name-derived frame, but their area should not compete with the center.
- A band near 6.5% of enclosure height keeps the Cs legible while making them structurally secondary.
- The central orbit may grow slightly, but the approved spark remains unchanged.
- Palette, wide aspect ratio and square assembled-logo allocation remain stable.
- The outer frame should also belong to the same family as the wordmark: angular 45-degree cuts and clipped joins, without copying any individual letter.
