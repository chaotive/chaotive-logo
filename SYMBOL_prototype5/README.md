## Direction

Rebalance the mark so the Creative Chaos idea becomes the primary read and the connected Cs act as a supporting frame.

- The wide 3:2 enclosure and exact brand palette remain unchanged.
- C-band thickness falls from roughly 13% to about 6.5% of the enclosure height.
- Rounded center joins are replaced by restrained 45-degree chamfers and clipped red/navy transitions that echo the angular CHAOTIVE letterforms.
- The circular orbit grows modestly from radius 155 to 170.
- Orbit weight increases slightly so the center holds up beside the long outer frame.
- The approved spark shape, size and exact center remain unchanged.
- Three planets remain directly seated on the continuous orbit at balanced intervals.
- The result follows a common game-brand hierarchy: distinctive internal idea first, light structural container second.
