# Prototype 4 — Opposing CC system

Prototype 4 refines the Creative Chaos monogram into a more self-contained graphical mark.

- The crimson C faces inward from the left.
- The navy C is horizontally mirrored and faces inward from the right.
- Their opposing openings create a central field of visual tension while preserving two readable C forms.
- The creative spark and chaos orbital fragments remain supporting details.
- CHAOTIVE is smaller, centered, and approximately the same width as the emblem.
- All wordmark letters share the same scale. Crimson and navy alternate from letter to letter, removing the earlier emphasis on a standalone red A.

The palette remains limited to the established crimson and deep navy. A third color was avoided because the alternating two-color rhythm already connects the emblem and wordmark while preserving the identity's existing recognition.

Files:

- `chaotive-logo-v4.png`: transparent square PNG.
- `logo-showcase.html`: self-contained application showcase.
- `generation-prompt.txt`: exact built-in image-generation prompt.

This is a raster identity prototype. A selected direction should eventually be redrawn as vector artwork for exact geometry, kerning, optical balance, and dependable one-color variants.
