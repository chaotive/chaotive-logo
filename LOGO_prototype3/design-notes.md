# Prototype 3 — Creative Chaos monogram

Prototype 3 replaces prototype 2's large CA construction with a clear CC monogram for Creative Chaos.

- The crimson C contains a four-point spark: a compact symbol for creative ignition.
- The deep navy C opens into an orbital cluster: displaced fragments represent chaos and unpredictable motion.
- Rounded corners, blunt terminals, and broad curves soften the earlier prototypes' aggressive edges.
- The lower CHAOTIVE signature returns to prototype 1's idea, with the oversized crimson A integrated into the dark wordmark.
- The emblem and wordmark are stacked as a naturally square identity.

Files:

- `chaotive-logo-v3.png`: transparent square PNG.
- `logo-showcase.html`: self-contained browser showcase with embedded artwork.
- `generation-prompt.txt`: exact built-in image-generation prompt.

This is a raster identity prototype. The chosen direction should ultimately be redrawn as vector artwork to refine curves, spacing, symbol geometry, and small-size behavior.
