# Prototype 2 — square-first identity

An evolution of prototype 1 for Chaotive, a solo videogame studio whose name means creative chaos.

Retain the crimson A, near-black navy, angular character, and deliberate collisions. Modernize through simpler geometry and a compact A/C emblem above the full CHAOTIVE name. Design the overall silhouette for square images from the beginning, rather than padding a horizontal wordmark.

- `chaotive-logo-v2.png`: generated square identity concept, transparent PNG.
- `logo-showcase.html`: self-contained application showcase with light and dark surfaces and interactive scale/background controls.
- `generation-prompt.txt`: exact prompt used with the built-in image generation tool.

This remains a raster design prototype; small-size legibility and final vector artwork should be refined when a direction is selected. The full name will need more space than the emblem alone at avatar sizes.
