# Prototype 6 — Design notes

The symbol is built as one connected enclosure divided by color. The red and navy C bodies share central top and bottom joints, allowing the silhouette to read as a single emblem while the color seam reveals the two component letters.

The right C is horizontally reversed. Both openings therefore face the center, creating a contained field for the supporting symbols.

The internal graphics are deliberately reduced:

- Chaos uses one broken orbital arc and three circular nodes.
- Creativity uses one primary four-point spark and one secondary spark.

The CHAO/TIVE wordmark and its color split remain the established prototype 5 direction.

This is a raster design prototype. A vector redraw should remove remaining generation artifacts, mathematically equalize symbol and wordmark bounds, and define exact joint, orbit, and spark geometry.
