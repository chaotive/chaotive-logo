# Prototype 6 — Connected opposing CC

- **Version:** 6
- **Created:** 2026-10-31
- **Based on:** Prototype 5
- **Status:** Preserved legacy full-logo exploration

## Direction

Prototype 6 preserves prototype 5's CHAO/TIVE wordmark and focuses on making the CC symbol easier to understand as one graphical object.

- The crimson left C opens right and represents chaos.
- The navy right C is mirrored, opens left, and represents creativity.
- Shared top and bottom joints connect both Cs into one continuous two-color emblem.
- A simplified three-node orbit sits inside the chaos C.
- A large spark and one smaller spark sit inside the creativity C.
- The C bodies define a clean outer boundary; supporting graphics remain inside the emblem.

## Changes from prototype 5

- Reversed the right C so the two openings face one another.
- Connected both Cs structurally at the top and bottom.
- Moved the orbit completely inside the crimson C.
- Simplified the orbit and creativity sparks for small-size recognition.
- Expanded the connected emblem to track the wordmark width more closely.
